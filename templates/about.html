AI Student Helper

Your intelligent academic companion — powered by advanced AI to help you learn faster, understand deeper, and achieve more.

AI Student Helper is a modern educational assistant built to support students across all academic subjects. Whether you're working through complex mathematics, drafting essays, analyzing historical events, or learning a new programming language — the assistant guides you step by step.

Powered by cutting-edge AI models including Groq Llama 3 and Google Gemini — plus support for your own Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, or Groq API keys — the app delivers fast, accurate, and contextually tailored explanations for your learning style.

Built with a warm, distraction-free reading and writing workspace, AI Student Helper keeps the focus on what matters most: understanding concepts deeply and building confidence.

Key Features

Built to help students study smarter across every subject.

Lightning Fast

Groq's high-speed Llama 3 inference and Google Gemini deliver step-by-step explanations with natural pacing, LaTeX math rendering, and syntax-highlighted code blocks.

Multi-Subject AI

Tailored prompts for Math, Science, English, History, Computer Science, and general coursework — complete with customizable Grade Level, Answer Length, and AI Tone.

Persistent Chat History

Your questions and step-by-step answers are saved to PostgreSQL so you can search, filter by subject, and revisit past study sessions from any device.

Bring Your Own API Key

Want to use your own Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT-4o, or Groq account? Connect your personal API key in Settings and pick the exact model you want.
