Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: November 2026

Student-First Privacy Commitment

We collect only the minimum data required to save your study history and preferences — never selling or sharing your personal data with advertisers.

AI Student Helper (built and maintained by Mohammad) is an educational study companion designed to help students learn across Mathematics, Science, Computer Science, History, and English. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is stored in our PostgreSQL database, and how you stay in full control of your data.

Account Information

When you sign up via Email or Google OAuth, we store your email address, first and last name, and hashed password (for email accounts) in our PostgreSQL User table.

Study & Chat History

When "Auto-save conversations" is enabled in Settings, your questions, AI responses, subject tags, and timestamps are stored in the PostgreSQL Message table so you can revisit past study sessions.

Learning Preferences

Your preferred default subject, grade level, explanation style, theme (dark/light), and text size are saved to your account profile so your workspace stays personalized across devices.

What We Never Do

We never sell student data, display third-party behavioral ads, or share your personal account records with data brokers.

Bring Your Own API Key (BYOK) & Security

How your personal AI provider keys and account credentials are protected at rest and in transit.

Encrypted at Rest (PBKDF2-SHA256 + HMAC-SHA256)
If you connect your own Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Groq API key in Settings, your key is encrypted using a per-user key derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with an HMAC-SHA256 authentication tag before being stored in our server-side PostgreSQL database.
Server-Side Vault
Never Exposed in Browser Cookies or DOM
Your plaintext or encrypted API key is never stored in client-side browser cookies, localStorage, or rendered HTML. Only a masked preview (for example, AIza••••••••••••7f9a) is shown in the Settings UI, and the input field is scrubbed from browser memory immediately after verification.
Zero Console Leak
Header-Only Provider Requests
When generating answers or verifying your key with Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or Groq, your API key is transmitted strictly over HTTPS request headers — never in query-string URLs or server error logs.
HTTPS Headers
Password Hashing
For email and password accounts, passwords are never stored in plaintext. All passwords are salted and hashed using Werkzeug's cryptographic password hashing before database storage.
Salted Hash

Google OAuth 2.0 & Session Cookies

How Google Sign-In and essential session cookies work in AI Student Helper.

Google OAuth 2.0 Sign-In: When you choose "Continue with Google", we request only the basic OpenID scopes (openid, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile). We receive your verified email address, display name, and profile avatar URL to create or sign into your AI Student Helper account. We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or Calendar.

Essential Session Cookies: AI Student Helper uses a cryptographically signed Flask session cookie to keep you signed in and remember your active subject and theme preferences while navigating the app. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Third-Party AI Processing: When you submit a study question, the question text and recent conversation context (up to the last 10 turns in that chat) are sent to the selected AI provider (Groq, Google Gemini, Anthropic, or OpenAI) solely to generate your tutoring response.
